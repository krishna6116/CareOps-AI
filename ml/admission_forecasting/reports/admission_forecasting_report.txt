--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This forecasting model is an operations decision-support component. It should not be represented as an autonomous clinical diagnosis or treatment system. The public portfolio implementation should use synthetic/de-identified data. Forecasts should support qualified healthcare operations professionals rather than silently automate decisions.
