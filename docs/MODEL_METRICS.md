## How the models are evaluated

- **Time-based splits only.** Models never see the holdout during training or model selection.
- **Warm-up excluded.** The simulated hospital opens empty; the first 21 days (before 2024-01-22) are not used for training, validation or evaluation.
- **Time series (admissions, occupancy, ICU).** From 24 weekly origins (2026-03-27 to 2026-09-04) every candidate forecasts the next 30 days using only data up to the origin. Learned models forecast recursively, as in production. Baselines: last observed value, same weekday last week, 7- and 28-day moving averages. The lowest MAE is published.
- **Length of stay.** Predicted at admission. The model and the baselines (training mean, department mean, department x severity mean) are fitted on stays already discharged before the holdout and scored on the admissions in each rolling 30-day window. The lowest pooled MAE is published.
- **No-show.** Scored the day before the appointment. Calibrated XGBoost, logistic regression and the training no-show rate are scored on each rolling 30-day window; the lowest pooled Brier score is published. Thresholds for precision/recall/F1 are chosen on validation data.
- **Metrics.** MAE and RMSE; MAPE for LOS (no zero stays); sMAPE and MASE for count series with zeros (MASE below 1 beats the in-sample same-weekday forecast). Classification: precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Brier, log loss and expected calibration error (ECE).
- **Prediction intervals.** Conformal absolute-error quantiles by horizon (days 1-7, 8-14, 15-30). Coverage is now measured as published: at each origin the width uses only errors already observed (expanding window) and the whole-number interval is checked. The earlier split-half check on the raw interval is shown for comparison.
- **Leakage audit.** `tests/test_leakage_audit.py` perturbs values at time t and checks that no feature at or before t changes, for every model.
